# Google sign-in assets

Retrieved on 2026-10-21 from Google's official sources.

- `g-logo.png`: the unmodified gradient Google G provided on the [Google Identity branding page](https://developers.google.com/identity/branding-guidelines), downloaded from https://developers.google.com/static/identity/images/g-logo.png.
- `google-sans-medium-latin.woff2` and `google-sans-medium-latin-ext.woff2`: Google Sans Medium (weight 500), downloaded from the Google Fonts stylesheet at https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Google+Sans:wght@500&display=swap. The two subsets cover the app's supported interface languages.
- `OFL.txt`: the font's SIL Open Font License, preserved from https://github.com/googlefonts/googlesans/blob/main/OFL.txt.

The button follows Google's light rectangular button specifications: white background, `#747775` border, `#1f1f1f` text, Google Sans Medium at 14px/20px, a 20px mark, 12px horizontal padding, and 10px between the mark and text. The text is localized using the app's existing translations, as permitted by the branding guidelines. The Google mark remains in its original colors and aspect ratio.

The Google mark is a Google brand asset; the font's open-source license does not grant rights to Google trademarks.
