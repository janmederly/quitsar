# Generated biology illustrations

Generated with the built-in `image_gen` tool. The selected outputs were resized and encoded as WebP for the app's existing image limits. The original generated PNG files are retained in the image generator's output folder.

## animal-cell.webp

```text
Use case: scientific-educational
Asset type: high-school biology quiz illustration for a web app.
Primary request: Create a scientifically clear, attractive textbook illustration of a typical animal cell in cutaway, landscape 3:2 composition, on a clean pale neutral background. Use softly shaded, realistic educational illustration rather than flat geometric placeholders. Include a large purple nucleus with a darker nucleolus, a pale blue cytoplasm, a distinct thin enclosing plasma membrane, and a few recognizable small mitochondria and ribosomes. No cell wall or chloroplasts.
Labels: exactly three large readable dark letters "A", "B", "C" outside the cell, each with a thin precise leader line. A points unambiguously to the main purple nucleus (not the nucleolus), B points to the thin outer plasma membrane, C points to an empty area of cytoplasm between organelles. Keep each line endpoint visible. No arrows should cross an unrelated structure.
Text (verbatim): "A", "B", "C". No other text, names, titles, answer hints, legend, watermark, or branding. The question will ask students to identify A; do not name it in the illustration.
Constraints: anatomically plausible high-school schematic, strong legibility at a displayed width of 300px, uncluttered, no excessive tiny organelle detail, no decorative background, no photographic microscopy style, no green cast.
```

## food-chain.webp

```text
Use case: scientific-educational
Asset type: high-school biology quiz illustration for a web app.
Primary request: A clean, scientifically accurate food-chain illustration in a very wide landscape composition on a plain off-white background. Four recognizable organisms in one horizontal row: a clump of living grass, a grasshopper, a frog, and a slender grass snake. Clearly separated with ample space, all fully visible and equal visual prominence. Three simple dark arrows in the gaps point from grass to grasshopper, grasshopper to frog, and frog to snake, showing energy transfer from food to eater. No circular arrows or extra links.
Style/medium: polished natural-history textbook illustrations with soft realistic shading and accurate anatomy, simple enough to read on a phone. Grasshopper has six legs; frog is a frog, not a toad; snake is a snake with no legs. Natural colors within the organisms, neutral background.
Text: none. No organism names, labels, titles, captions, answer hints, watermark, or branding. The app supplies the question and accessibility description separately.
Constraints: exactly these four organisms, exactly three right-pointing arrows, no sun or scenery, no decorative elements, no additional animals, readable at small scale.
```
